    WHAT IT DOES

    Finds the hidden data, shows it, then removes it

    AI labels and prompts

    C2PA / Content Credentials, IPTC "made by AI" tags, and traces from ChatGPT / DALL-E, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, Google Gemini, Stable Diffusion and ComfyUI prompts.

    Location and hidden data

    EXIF data such as GPS location, phone model and dates, plus XMP and IPTC blocks, comments and data hidden after the end of the image. It warns you when a photo shows where it was taken.

    GOOD TO KNOW

    Questions

    Are my images uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything runs in your browser on your own device. The page is not even allowed to make network requests, so your images stay with you even if you lose your connection while using it.

    Does it change the quality of my images?

    Not with the default "Same as original" and "Original size" settings: for JPG, PNG and WebP the tool cuts out only the metadata and copies the image data unchanged. Other formats (HEIC, AVIF, GIF, BMP) are opened by your browser and saved as a new JPG or PNG, which also drops all metadata.

    Can I resize or convert images at the same time?

    Yes. Under "Output", pick a format (JPG, PNG or WebP), a size and a quality. The images you already added are processed again with the new settings. Resized or converted images are saved fresh, so they never carry the old metadata.

    Can it remove invisible AI watermarks like SynthID?

    No. Some AI tools hide a watermark inside the pixels themselves. That is not metadata, so no metadata remover can take it out. This tool removes the labels and data stored around the image.

    How do I know it worked?

    After cleaning, each file is scanned again. "Clean" means no AI signal and no removable metadata was found in the new file. Open "Details" to see exactly what was found and removed.
